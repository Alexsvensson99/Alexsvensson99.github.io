Who is it for?

For creators and editors with existing SRT or WebVTT files who want to review and adjust them locally.

What you can do

  • Open SRT and WebVTT files with up to 5,000 cues and 5 MiB per file.
  • Review exact millisecond timing and adjust selected cues with offsets, reflow, or empty-line proposals.
  • Use editable readability profiles as your own review preferences.
  • Save portable projects and export a review CSV while keeping the source beside the current draft.

How it works

  1. 01

    Open START.html and import an existing SRT or WebVTT file.

  2. 02

    Review the findings and make selected cue-level changes.

  3. 03

    Resolve negative or reversed timings that block export, then export in the format you choose.

Frequently asked questions

Can I work offline?

Yes. Open START.html from the downloaded folder. You need no account, server, or Node.

Does it create captions from audio or video?

No. It reviews SRT and WebVTT files you already have.

Can every WebVTT file be converted to SRT?

No. The tool prevents a conversion when it would lose information it is preserving.

What is included

  • The local CaptionWeave app
  • SRT and WebVTT support with exact millisecond timing
  • Undo/redo, selected-cue offsets, and editable review profiles
  • Portable JSON project and review CSV export
  • Quick-start guide, demo material, and synthetic examples

Before you buy

  • CaptionWeave does not create captions from audio or video and does not translate. Readability profiles are editable preferences, not universal standards. Negative or reversed timings remain visible and block subtitle export until resolved; repeated captions are never silently merged. The tool prevents WebVTT-to-SRT conversion or edits that would lose preserved metadata, markup, entities, cue settings, or unknown structures. Tested locally on macOS in Chrome and Safari; other environments are not verified.
