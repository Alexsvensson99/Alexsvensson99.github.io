Frequently asked questions

Can I work offline?

Yes. Open START.html from the downloaded folder. No account, server, or cloud connection is required.

Can I revise a project later?

Yes. Save the portable project and reopen it in a compatible browser.

Why is export blocked?

Each selected combination needs complete copy that fits its layout. Correct the reported copy or layout, then export again.
