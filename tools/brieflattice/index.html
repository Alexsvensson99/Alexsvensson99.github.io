Who is it for?

For designers and marketers who need a focused local workflow for a small image campaign.

What you can do

  • Use three fixed layouts for product messages, events, or quotes.
  • Export square, portrait, story, and landscape versions from the same campaign.
  • Set Cover or Contain, crop focus, and text placement per variant and format.
  • Review selected combinations in a contact sheet before creating a PNG or JPEG ZIP.

How it works

  1. 01

    Open START.html and import local images plus UTF-8 CSV copy.

  2. 02

    Choose a layout and adjust the composition for each variant and format.

  3. 03

    Review the contact sheet and export the ordered ZIP file.

Frequently asked questions

Can I reuse a campaign?

Yes. Save the portable JSON project and reopen it in a compatible browser.

What happens if the text is too long?

The affected composition is reported and export remains blocked until you shorten the copy or adjust the layout or placement.

Are these dimensions approved by every platform?

No. They are the product's supported pixel dimensions. Always check the current platform requirements.

What is included

  • The local BriefLattice app
  • Three fixed layouts: product, event, and quote
  • Four pixel formats: 1080×1080, 1080×1350, 1080×1920, and 1920×1080
  • PNG or JPEG ZIP export with a manifest
  • Portable JSON project, quick-start guide, and fictional example material

Before you buy

  • BriefLattice is not a freeform design editor, image or copy generator, cloud storage service, publishing scheduler, or ad-platform connection. It supports up to 25 source images and 25 variants. Export is blocked when copy does not fit, and output dimensions are pixels, not platform guarantees. Tested locally on macOS in Chrome and Safari; other environments are not verified.
