Who is it for?

For web designers and agencies handing over responsibilities, open questions, and practical instructions in a selected client package.

What you can do

  • Collect a project overview, ownership, open questions, instructions, and support scope locally.
  • Choose exactly which sections, checklist items, and attachments the client receives.
  • Keep internal notes and comments out of the customer export.
  • Create a standalone package with a handbook, Markdown, CSV, and selected instruction images.

How it works

  1. 01

    Open START.html and enter the project's client-relevant information.

  2. 02

    Choose sections, checklists, and attachments for the client version.

  3. 03

    Export the ZIP package and open the handbook locally; use Print when a PDF is needed.

Frequently asked questions

Can I control what the client sees?

Yes. Select sections, checklist items, and attachments before export.

Is my private project included in the ZIP?

No. The private project is saved separately and remains outside the exported client package.

Can I create a PDF handbook?

Yes. Open START-HANDBOOK.html from the package and use Print or Save as PDF.

Before you buy

  • Relayfolio does not scan or certify a website, security, backups, launch readiness, or an agreement. It has no password or secret fields; its possible-secret warning is limited and must be reviewed before sharing. Store up to eight instruction images and one logo, and keep the portable project within 40 MiB. The WordPress templates are version-neutral starting points, so check current official instructions for the actual site. Tested locally on macOS in Chrome and Safari; other environments are not verified.
