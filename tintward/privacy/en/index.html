Responsibility and contact

Tintward is developed by Alexander Svensson. You can contact me about the app, this policy or information you have sent to support at alex@svensson.design.

This policy describes Tintward 1.0 for iPhone and iPad and this support website. The app is in development and is not yet published on the App Store.

Images and colors

You choose which images and files Tintward can open through the system pickers. Images are processed on your device and their colors are converted to sRGB locally. The original image is not saved in the palette library. The displayed image and selected filename are held in memory during use.

The app does not send your images, colors or palette names to me. It has no sign-in, advertising, analytics, tracking or networking service of its own.

Library and recovery

Your palette library is stored in the app's local storage. It includes palette names, light and dark color values, dates, local palette identifiers, and settings for the original color, lock, intended use and manual edits. Identifiers belong to palettes, not people or devices.

The last valid library file is kept as a previous version for recovery. An explicit restore from a backup also preserves the replaced file as a separate local recovery copy. These recovery copies are not deleted automatically.

Deleting a palette removes it from the current library. A copy may remain temporarily in the undo function, in previous library snapshots and recovery copies, or in exported backups.

Clipboard, export and sharing

Tintward writes a HEX code or export data to the clipboard when you choose Copy. It does not read the clipboard in the background. Other apps and system clipboard features then handle the content according to your device settings.

CSS, SwiftUI and Xcode exports contain theme colors. JSON backups also contain palette names and library metadata. You initiate export and sharing. The location, app or service you choose then handles the content.

Sharing creates a temporary file inside the app. The latest file for each export format may remain until replaced, cleared by the system or removed when you delete the app. Exported backups are readable JSON files, not encrypted archives created by Tintward.

System services and cloud files

Tintward has no cloud synchronization of its own. If you select an image or file in a cloud service, Photos or its system file provider may need to download it. That follows the service's terms and your settings.

Device backups may include app data. Apple's system diagnostics, the App Store and the services you choose for sharing handle information under their own terms. Tintward has no crash-reporting or payment-processing service of its own.

Deleting and keeping your data

You can delete palettes in the library. To remove the app's local library, recovery copies and temporary files, delete the app through iOS or iPadOS. Offloading an app is different from deleting its data. Save your own backup first if you want to keep your palettes.

Deleting the app does not remove files already exported elsewhere or earlier device backups. You manage those in their respective locations. Local protection comes from device and app storage protections; Tintward does not add its own password protection to backups.

When you contact support

If you email me, your email address, message and any attachments are sent to my email service. I use what you send to understand and respond to your request. This is separate from the app's local color processing.

Send only what is needed and avoid sensitive information. Use the same contact address to ask about, correct or request deletion of support information. Do not send your image library or all your palettes when a color code or description is enough.

This website

These Tintward pages use no analytics, external fonts, forms or JavaScript and set no cookies of their own. Other parts of svensson.design may have different features and their own analytics choices.

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs and stores visitors' IP addresses for security purposes, even when a visitor is not signed in to GitHub. This is the host's handling of a page visit, not data collection in the Tintward app.

Read the GitHub Privacy Statement and GitHub's information about Pages and visitor data.

Changes to this policy

If the way Tintward or this website handles information changes, the text and date on this page will be updated. Contact me if anything is unclear.
